Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • Last Updated: October 24, 2026

At RecallLC, we prioritize complete honesty with our users. This Refund and Cancellation Policy outlines how contributions, future subscriptions, and billing inquiries are handled by Pranav Sharma ("we", "our").

📌 Current Status Overview

Core Extension: RecallLC is currently 100% free and open-source. There are no fees or billing requirements to use the extension.

Payment Gateway Integration: Payments, voluntary contributions, and Pro subscriptions are securely processed through Razorpay (and direct UPI). Razorpay is PCI-DSS compliant and supports Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking, UPI, and major Indian wallets.

1. Voluntary Developer Contributions (Tips / Donations)

If you choose to support the ongoing development of RecallLC through voluntary contributions (such as GitHub Sponsors, Buy Me A Coffee, or direct UPI support):

  • Nature of Payment: Voluntary contributions are gifts to support free open-source software and server-less maintenance. They do not constitute the purchase of commercial licenses or special privileges.
  • Mistaken or Duplicate Transactions: If you made a duplicate payment or entered an incorrect amount by mistake, please contact us at pranav2410991479@gmail.com within 7 days with your transaction reference (UTR / Order ID). We will review and initiate a full refund to your original payment method.

2. Commercial / Paid Services (Razorpay / Pro Tier)

When purchasing paid tiers (such as optional end-to-end encrypted multi-device cloud backup) or supporter tiers:

A. Cancellation Process

Users can cancel any recurring subscription at any time directly through their dashboard settings. Upon cancellation, access to paid features will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

B. Refund Eligibility

We offer a 14-day refund window for any initial paid plan purchase if the service does not meet your expectations or if technical defects prevent its use.

C. Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed via Razorpay directly to the original payment source (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or NetBanking) within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your issuing bank's settlement cycle.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for a mistaken transaction or billing inquiry, send an email to pranav2410991479@gmail.com with the following details:

  • Subject line: [RecallLC Refund Request] - Your Name
  • Date and exact amount of the transaction
  • Transaction ID / Payment Reference Number (UTR)
  • Brief reason for the refund request

We review all requests within 48 hours and treat our users fairly.
