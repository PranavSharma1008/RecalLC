Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • Last Updated: October 24, 2026

RecallLC ("we", "our", or "the extension") is developed by Pranav Sharma. We believe your study habits, problem-solving history, and code notes belong exclusively to you. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to a 100% local-first, zero-telemetry software architecture.

🔒 Summary of Privacy Guarantees

  • Zero Remote Servers: RecallLC does not transmit your revision data to any external server.
  • Zero Cookies or Credentials: We never ask for, read, store, or forward your LeetCode password or session cookies.
  • Zero Telemetry: No analytics, tracking pixels, or monitoring scripts are embedded in the extension.
  • Local Storage: All revision attempts, flashcards, and notes reside strictly inside your browser profile.

1. Permissions Requested & Their Purpose

RecallLC adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege. The extension manifest requests only the following permissions:

Permission Type Justification
storage API Permission Used to save your revision intervals, theme preference, and master passcode settings locally via chrome.storage.local.
https://leetcode.com/* Host Permission Enables the content script to detect problem URLs on leetcode.com/problems/*, render the in-page reminder, mount the inline Rocket button, and detect submit verdicts.

RecallLC explicitly does NOT request permissions such as tabs, cookies, webRequest, history, notifications, or <all_urls>.

2. Information Stored Locally

When you use RecallLC, the following information is generated and stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local:

  • Problem Identifiers: Problem title, slug (e.g. two-sum), difficulty level, and topic tags.
  • Revision Records: Timestamps of attempts, user recall rating (Forgot, Hard, Good, Easy), and calculated next due dates.
  • Submission Metadata: Submission verdicts (Accepted, Wrong Answer, TLE), language used (if detected), and submission timestamp.
  • User Notes: Any conceptual notes, time/space complexities, or edge cases you enter on the Problems or Recall screens.
  • Security Settings: Optional master passcode salt and hash (processed using Web Crypto SHA-256; plain-text passwords are never stored).

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties. No data leaves your machine.

When you interact with LeetCode (e.g. submitting code or loading a problem), standard web requests are handled directly by your browser to LeetCode under your own existing session. RecallLC does not inspect or intercept your session tokens.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored on your local machine, you retain complete control over your data at all times:

  • Export: You can export your full revision history to standard JSON or CSV anytime via the Dashboard > Settings screen.
  • Clear All Data: You can wipe all problems, revision logs, and attempts with one click via Dashboard > Settings > Clear All Data.
  • Uninstall: Removing the extension from chrome://extensions immediately deletes all associated extension-origin IndexedDB data.

5. Website Privacy (Landing Page)

This landing website (recalllc.netlify.app) is a static website hosted on Netlify. Netlify may collect standard server access logs (including visitor IP addresses, browser user-agent, and requested URLs) strictly for operational security and DDoS protection as outlined in Netlify's Privacy Policy. No cookies or marketing trackers are used on this website.
